Take the guided tour of the Old House at Peace field
Allow about 60–90 minutes.
11:45 a.m. – Visit the Birthplaces
Head to the nearby John Adams Birthplace and John Quincy Adams Birthplace at Penn's Hill.
1:00 p.m. – eat packed lunch 
Enjoy lunch in Quincy Center. There are many casual cafés and pubs within walking distance of the visitor center.
2:30 p.m. – United First Parish Church
Visit United First Parish Church, where both John Adams and John Quincy Adams are buried in the family crypt.
Plan for about 30–45 minutes.
3:30 p.m. – Stroll Quincy Center
Take a leisurely walk through the historic downtown, browse local shops, or relax with coffee before heading home.
